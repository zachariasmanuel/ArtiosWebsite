Villa Lake Mountain by Artios

5 Bedroom Private Lakeview Villa

1 / 10

24/7 Security Private Villa Pet Friendly Lake View

Welcome to Villa Lake Mountain by Artios, a peaceful lakeview villa surrounded by acres of private garden and forest in the heart of Weligama. Located just 5 minutes from Kai Beach, this beautiful nature stay offers calm surroundings, lake access, private decks, and spacious living areas perfect for families, groups, and long relaxing stays.The villa can be booked as a full 5-bedroom property for up to 10 guests or separately as Upper Villa and Ground Villa.

About the Villa

  • Two Private Villa Spaces: The property includes an Upper Villa and Ground Villa which can be booked together or separately depending on group size and preference.
  • Upper Villa: Features 3 A/C double bedrooms accommodating up to 6 guests and 1 non-A/C single bedroom with ceiling fan for 1 guest.
  • Ground Villa: Includes 1 A/C double bedroom for 2 guests and 1 A/C single bedroom for 1 guest.
  • Bathroom Layout: Upper Villa offers 2 attached bathrooms and 1 common bathroom shared between one double bedroom and one single bedroom. Ground Villa includes 1 common bathroom shared by the double bedroom and single bedroom.
  • Private Kitchens: Both Upper and Ground Villa include fully equipped private kitchen spaces for comfortable stays and light cooking.
  • Lake View & Lake Access: Enjoy peaceful lake views directly from the property along with private lake access surrounded by nature.
  • Private Garden & Forest Area: The villa is built on huge acres of private land with spacious gardens, forest areas behind the property, and peaceful outdoor seating spaces.
  • Private Decks & Sunbeds: Relax in outdoor seating areas with sunbeds and private decks overlooking greenery and lake surroundings.
  • Hospitality & Dining: Complimentary tea, coffee, and sugar are provided for guests during the stay.
  • Dedicated Caretaker: A caretaker is available from morning till night to assist guests with transport, local guidance, and stay requirements.

House Rules

  • Check-in: After 2 PM and Check-out: Before 11 AM
  • Only registered guests are allowed; no additional visitors or day guests.
  • Parties and loud music are not permitted beyond 10 PM.
  • Alcohol consumption is allowed in moderation.
  • Strictly no smoking inside the villa/rooms.
  • Illegal activities, including drug use or possession of firearms, are prohibited and will be reported to authorities.
  • Guests are responsible for their personal belongings; management is not liable for any loss or damage.
  • A valid government-issued photo ID (Aadhar, Driving License, or Passport) is mandatory for all guests at check-in.
  • Violation of these rules may result in cancellation of stay without a refund.
  • Security Deposit: ₹3,000 per villa (refundable).

Cancellation Policy

  • If booking is cancelled 21 days before checkin date, guests are entitled to a full refund. While, 50% refund if cancelled between 21-11 days before checkin date
  • Any cancellations made less than 11 days before your checkin date, would not be refunded.
  • If the Guest has made a partial payment, they are entitled to no refund whatsoever.
  • The guest is not entitled to any refund while rescheduling is possible*
  • Cancellation for peak dates, long weekends, Christmas & New Year will not be accepted.

Things to Know Before Your Stay

  • You will be staying in a peaceful private lakeview villa surrounded by gardens, forest, and nature.
  • Full villa can accommodate up to 10 guests. Upper Villa and Ground Villa can also be booked separately.
  • The property includes lake access, private decks, sunbeds, spacious garden areas, and peaceful outdoor seating spaces.
  • Located in Weligama, around 5 minutes drive from Kai Beach and close to nearby beaches, cafes, and restaurants.
  • This is a calm and relaxing nature stay perfect for families, groups, workations, and long stays.
  • During monsoon season, power outages and internet disruptions may occur due to weather conditions. Fluctuations are beyond our control.
